“Tell me about yourself” isn’t an invitation to recite your CV. The interviewer wants a short story: who you are at work, what you’re great at, and why this role is your next step.

The 60-second structure

  1. 1Present — 20 secondsYour role now and the one result you’re proudest of.
  2. 2Past — 20 secondsHow you got here: two experiences that built the skills this role needs.
  3. 3Future — 20 secondsWhy this company and this role are the natural next step.
“I’m a product designer at a fintech, where I led the design system now used by six teams. Before that I designed payments flows at an agency. I’m looking for a team where systems and payments meet — which is why this role caught my eye.”

Tip

Write it down, then say it out loud five times. It should sound like you, not like a script.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Starting with where you were born or studied.
  • Listing every job in order.
  • Talking for more than two minutes.
  • Ending without saying why this role.

Make it fit the role

Pick the result and the two experiences that match the job description best. A tailored answer works like a tailored CV — and Tau already builds that for every role.

Frequently asked questions

How long should my answer be?+

About 60 seconds — 90 at most. They’ll ask follow-up questions about what interests them.

Should I mention personal hobbies?+

Only if it’s one line and connects to the role. Keep the focus on work.

Is it the same as “walk me through your CV”?+

Similar, but more chronological. Use the same story and add one or two more steps.
