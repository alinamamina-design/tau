Tailoring your CV for every role — without making things up

Applicant tracking systems read keywords. Recruiters read stories. A good tailored CV works for both.

Sending the same CV everywhere is the most common reason good candidates get filtered out. Tailoring doesn’t mean inventing — it means choosing what to show first.

Mirror the language

If the role says “design systems”, don’t call yours a “component library”. Use their words for the work you really did.

Lead with the closest match

Move the most relevant project to the top of each role. Cut the bullets that don’t serve this application.

Keep it true

Every line should survive an interview question. If you can’t talk about it for two minutes, it doesn’t belong.
