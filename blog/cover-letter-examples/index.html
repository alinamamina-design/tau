Most cover letters repeat the CV. The ones that get read are short, specific and about the company. Here’s a template.

Hiring managers don’t read every cover letter — but when two candidates look alike, a good one decides who gets the call.

A three-paragraph template

  1. 1Why themOne specific reason: a product, a launch, a problem they’re solving.
  2. 2Why youYour closest match to the role, with one number.
  3. 3The askA short, confident close: you’d love to talk about how you can help with X.
“Your new payments onboarding is the smoothest I’ve used — I’d love to help take it further. At Fernway I redesigned onboarding and lifted activation by 18%.”

Tip

Keep it under 200 words. If it doesn’t fit on a phone screen, it’s too long.

  • You’d only be repeating your resume.

Frequently asked questions

Do recruiters still read cover letters?+

Some do, especially for smaller teams and competitive roles. A short, specific one never hurts.

Can I reuse one cover letter?+

Reuse the structure, not the text. The first paragraph must be about this company.
