Few LinkedIn features split opinion like the green #OpenToWork frame. Some recruiters say it makes their job easier. Others admit it makes them wonder why you need it.

Two ways to be “open”

  1. 1Recruiters onlyVisible to people using LinkedIn Recruiter — not to your current employer’s recruiters. No green frame.
  2. 2All LinkedIn membersAdds the green frame to your photo. Everyone sees it, including your manager.

Tip

If you’re still employed, use “Recruiters only”. You get found without telling your whole network.

When the green frame helps

  • You were laid off and want your network to help.
  • You’re early in your career or changing fields.
  • You’re open to many roles in one city.

When to skip it

  • You’re employed and searching quietly.
  • You’re senior and want to be approached, not seen as available.
  • Your headline already says what you’re looking for.

Either way, the banner is passive. Most offers still come from applying early and messaging the people who are hiring.

Frequently asked questions

Can my employer see “Open to Work”?+

With “Recruiters only”, LinkedIn tries to hide it from recruiters at your company — but it can’t guarantee it.

Does the banner get more recruiter messages?+

It can, mostly for junior and mid-level roles. Senior candidates often get more from a clear headline.

What should I do instead of waiting?+

Apply in the first day a role is posted and reach out to the hiring manager. Tau does both for you.
